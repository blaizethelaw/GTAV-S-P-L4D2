Core Vision:
Keep the entire GTA V single-player map, campaign missions, weapon wheel, and vehicle physics intact, but seamlessly integrate the assets, weapons, mechanics, and enemies from Left 4 Dead 2, Skate, and Portal 2 directly into the open world of Los Santos.

1. Portal 2 Integration (Aperture Science Tech & Assets):

The Portal Gun: Add the Aperture Science Handheld Portal Device to the player's Weapon Wheel (Special/Heavy slot) immediately upon loading in. Fire linked Blue and Orange Portals on any static world geometry (walls, billboards, streets, buildings, rooftops).

Momentum & Teleportation: Maintain full velocity and vehicle/skateboard momentum when passing through linked portals.

Mandatory NPC & Enemy Physics ("The Tank vs. Portal Combo"): Keep full portal physics interactions enabled on all NPCs, vehicles, and Left 4 Dead 2 Special Infected. If the player fires an Orange Portal directly underneath a charging L4D2 Tank (or horde) linked to a Blue Portal at the top of the Maze Bank Tower, the Tank/NPCs must fall through the portal, inherit the terminal velocity, and ragdoll/launch out of the exit portal in real time.

Portal Assets & Gadgets: Spawn functional Aperture Science Sentry Turrets (with voice lines and laser sight targeting), Weighted Companion Cubes (physics-enabled, can be picked up or launched), Aerial Faith Plates on major Los Santos rooftops, and Repulsion/Propulsion/Conversion Gel bombs as throwable items in the grenade slot.

2. Skate Integration (Flick-It Board Mechanics & Spots):

Equipable Skateboard: Allow the player to toggle a custom skateboard on/off at any time.

Physics-Based Skating: Implement custom skate physics in GTA V—kickflips, ollies, manual balancing, and dynamic rail grinding on all Los Santos street curbs, handrails, ledges, and stairs.

Skate & Portal Synergy: Allow the player to stay locked onto their skateboard while flying through portals, riding over Propulsion/Repulsion gels, and launching off Aerial Faith Plates for rooftop-to-rooftop transfers.

Custom Skate Props: Populate key areas (Vespucci Beach, Legion Square, Downtown) with drop-in mega-ramps, quarter pipes, and grind rails.

3. Left 4 Dead 2 Integration (The Infected & Survivor Gear):

Dynamic Horde & Special Infected: Convert ambient Los Santos pedestrians in designated zones (or toggleable city-wide via an "Apocalypse Director" menu) into fast-sprinting L4D2 Common Infected. Periodically spawn Special Infected with their signature abilities, models, and audio cues: Tanks (throwing cars/concrete and vulnerable to portal traps), Chargers, Hunters, Smokers (pulling the player off their skateboard or out of cars), and Boomers.

L4D2 Arsenal & Healing: Add iconic L4D2 melee weapons (Frying Pan with authentic clang sound, Katana, Fire Axe, Chainsaw), L4D2 shove/bash mechanics, and throwables (Pipe Bomb that attracts infected, Bile Jar) to the weapon wheel. Add Medkits, Defibrillators, and Adrenaline Shots (temporarily boosting skate push speed and reload speed).

4. Strict Input & Control Mapping (PS5 Controller + Mouse & Keyboard):

Zero Input Conflicts: Because Skate relies heavily on thumbsticks for Flick-It tricks and Portal 2 relies on dual-trigger firing (L2/R2 on PS5 controller or Left-Click/Right-Click on Mouse), enforce strict context-sensitive input separation so "Equip Skateboard" or "Do Kickflip" NEVER clashes with "Fire Blue/Orange Portal" or standard GTA V aiming/driving controls:

PS5 DualSense Controller: When the Portal Gun is equipped on foot or on the skateboard, L2 fires the Blue Portal and R2 fires the Orange Portal (disabling standard GTA V aim-down-sights lock-on while holding the Portal Gun). Toggle the Skateboard on/off using D-Pad Right (Hold). Map L4D2 melee shove to Circle ( R3 when aiming).

Mouse & Keyboard: Left-Click fires Blue Portal; Right-Click fires Orange Portal. Toggle Skateboard on/off with the B key. Use Spacebar / Shift / Arrow Keys (or Mouse Gestures) for skate tricks and grinds, and F / Middle Mouse for L4D2 shove.
